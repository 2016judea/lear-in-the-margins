Classroom licence

The course PDF (King Lear or Hamlet). You may give the file to the enrolled students of one course you teach, for as long as you teach it. Post it on your course site or learning platform, print it for them, project it in class. Students may keep their copy.

Not covered. Posting it where anyone outside the course can download it, or selling it. A second course is a second licence.

A printed copy belongs to its owner, like any book.

What is ours to licence. The play is the Moby Shakespeare text, in the public domain. Keats's sonnet (1818) is in the public domain, as is Spurgeon's Keats's Shakespeare (1928). Milton's marks were located by eye in the Free Library of Philadelphia's facsimile of his First Folio. Keats's Hamlet underlinings were read from Harvard's Houghton Library digitization of his own copy. No image from either is reproduced, and the descriptions are ours. The licence covers the edition: the layout, the placement of each mark, and the notes.
