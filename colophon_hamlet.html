Course edition, 2026. Hamlet with the marks of John Milton and John Keats, each set beside the line that provoked it.

Play text. The Moby Shakespeare, as published at shakespeare.mit.edu, whose site places it in the public domain.

Milton. Twenty-four marks, fifteen of them words he wrote in the margin, located by eye in the Free Library of Philadelphia's digitization of its 1623 First Folio (archive.org/details/shk00001), identified as Milton's by Claire M. L. Bourne and Jason Scott-Warren (2019). No image from the facsimile is reproduced; the descriptions are ours.

Keats. Nineteen underlinings read from the Houghton Library's digitization of his own copy of The Dramatic Works (London, 1814–18), vol. 7, shelfmark *EC8 K2262 Zz814s, and checked against Caroline F. E. Spurgeon, Keats's Shakespeare (1928). No image is reproduced.
