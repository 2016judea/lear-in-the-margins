Course edition, 2026. King Lear with the marks of John Milton and John Keats, each set beside the line that provoked it.

Play text. The Moby Shakespeare, as published at shakespeare.mit.edu, whose site places it in the public domain.

Milton. Five marks located by eye in the Free Library of Philadelphia's digitization of its 1623 First Folio (archive.org/details/shk00001), identified as Milton's by Claire M. L. Bourne and Jason Scott-Warren (2019). No image from the facsimile is reproduced; the descriptions are ours.

Keats. His sonnet On Sitting Down to Read King Lear Once Again (1818), and the mark documented by Caroline F. E. Spurgeon, Keats's Shakespeare (1928).
